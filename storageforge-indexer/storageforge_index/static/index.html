        Copies on other hosts

        A folder with a copy on another host: each may be the backup of the other. Keep in sync prepares a plan in Moves that copies one side to the other on a timer. Nothing syncs until you deploy that plan there.

        Duplicate folders

        Folders that hold the same file names and sizes in the same layout, so they are likely copies, on one host or across hosts. Checksums are not needed for these; they confirm the identical files below. A copy on another host is a possible backup: it is listed apart and never counted as space to free.

        A second system beside the first

        Another distribution on a host you reach over SSH, in unpartitioned space or on a blank disk, with a boot entry of its own. The host fetches the distribution's cloud image and checks it against the checksum its publisher lists. The form makes a change plan: nothing runs until you have read it and run it in Changes.
