    Rules: what raises a notification

    A rule counts the lines it means as scans bring them in, and is one notification per host when it reaches its threshold. A pattern is text with * for anything and | between alternatives.

      Netconsole: kernel messages over UDP

      A host's kernel can send its messages straight to the controller, which still works when nothing else on the host does: a panic or a hang reaches here. They come over plain UDP with no signature. The controller takes them only from the address and port given here, and a line that came this way says something sent it from that address, not that the host did. The netconsole plan step sets a host up to send.
