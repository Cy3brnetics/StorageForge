# Code of conduct

StorageForge is a small project run by volunteers. The rule is simple: treat the people here the way you'd want to be treated by someone
who is helping you for free.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux, storage or networking. Everyone started somewhere.
- Disagree about the code, not the person. Say what you think is wrong and why, and accept that the answer may still be no.
- Assume good faith. Text loses tone; ask before you take offence.
- Respect people's time: search before you open an issue, and give enough detail for someone to reproduce what you saw.
- Keep secrets out of public threads: no passwords, keys, tokens or other people's host names and addresses. A security problem goes to a
  private report (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), not an issue.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or attacks on anyone for who they are: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
  experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or anything else.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, and repeating a point after the maintainer has decided.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer, Gabe (gabemurk on GitHub), privately, or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request.
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules,
starting with a warning unless the behaviour is serious.

This covers the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and wiki, and anywhere someone is acting on behalf of the project.
